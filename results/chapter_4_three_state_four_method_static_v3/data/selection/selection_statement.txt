Under the declared Trap-III geometry, ideal matched readout and detector model, DPFI gives the largest whole-image SNR for BEC, SSP and ID, while DGI is second for all three. DPFI is therefore continued as the signed port-difference readout with leading-order linear response, and DGI as the dark-background quadratic comparator. This is a configuration-specific scope decision, not a universal method ranking or a feature-recovery result.
