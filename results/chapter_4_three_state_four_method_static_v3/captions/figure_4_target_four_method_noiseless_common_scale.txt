Noiseless camera responses of the BEC, SSP and ID density profiles in PCI, DGI, DFFI and DPFI. PCI and DGI show blank-subtracted intensity, DFFI shows the crossed-polariser intensity, and DPFI shows the difference between the H and V analyser ports. Each quantity is divided by the same incident intensity I0, and all twelve panels share one zero-centred colour scale. Colour therefore compares the fractional detected response directly, including the optical gain of each readout, but does not compare noise or measurement precision. The signed DGI and DPFI responses are shown without clipping. In the ID profile, the phase of the stronger circular component exceeds pi locally; the forward images remain valid, but their intensity cannot be inverted globally to give a unique column density.
