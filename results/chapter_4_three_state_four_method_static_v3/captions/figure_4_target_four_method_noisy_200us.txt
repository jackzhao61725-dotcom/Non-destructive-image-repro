One 200-microsecond exposure of each BEC, SSP and ID density profile with PCI, DGI, DFFI and DPFI. Every panel uses the same incident probe dose and detector model, including photon shot noise and 0.7-electron read noise. DPFI is formed from two independently noisy H and V analyser ports, so subtracting their mean backgrounds does not remove their shot or read noise. All twelve panels are divided by the same incident intensity I0 and share one zero-centred colour scale, allowing their fractional detected responses to be compared directly. In the ID profile, the stronger circular phase exceeds pi locally, so image strength is not a globally single-valued measure of column density.
