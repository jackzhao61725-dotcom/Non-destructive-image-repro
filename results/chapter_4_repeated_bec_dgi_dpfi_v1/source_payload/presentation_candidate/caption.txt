\textbf{Whole-image template SNR and one noisy DPFI multiframe realisation for the target TFBEC.} (a,b) The DGI and DPFI heat maps show the deterministic whole-image template SNR over all 16 pulse durations and all 15 frames. Each method uses its own sequential scale from zero to that method's maximum; the horizontal dashed guides identify the three pulse-duration rows displayed below. (c) The DPFI montage shows one preselected simulated camera acquisition at $\tau=(25,200,400)\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ and odd $q=1,3,\ldots,15$. Each image is the dark-corrected $H-V$ atom-port difference normalised by the dark-corrected blank-port sum, followed only by a display sign of $-1$; it is not normalised by the atom-port $H+V$. All 24 images share one zero-centred symmetric colour scale, retain both signed tails, and are not clipped or rescaled by row or panel. The displayed acquisition is not presented as a typical outcome. Each value of $\tau$ starts a fresh independent sequence; frame $q$ is the completely re-equilibrated state after $q-1$ prior probe pulses. The same-model calculation contains no intermediate dynamics and does not establish an experimental probability or a universal DGI--DPFI ranking.
